Auto Client Outreach

Outreach

Send personalised emails to your whole client list from your own Gmail. Here’s how to use it, step by step:

  1. Upload your Excel sheet with your clients’ details (names, companies and their email addresses).
  2. State what kind of email you want to draft in the Describe the Email section: the subject, who it’s for, the headline, your opening line, what you offer and the button text.
  3. Upload your logo or banner in the Company Logo section. It goes at the top of every email.
  4. Give your company info in the Company Details section: company email, phone number, website, and the header and footer details.
  5. Connect Gmail, check the live preview, then create drafts or send. Nothing is sent until you press Send and confirm.

Used for the “Plain personal email” style and as the text fallback inside designed emails.

Every variable the template uses. A spreadsheet column with the same name always wins, so any of these can be personalised per row. Values can contain HTML and other merge fields.

How merge fields work
  • {{Company}} fills from the column “Company”. Case, spaces and underscores are ignored, so {{FIRST_NAME}} matches “First Name”.
  • {{First Name|there}} uses “there” when the cell is empty. {{Company:url}} URL-encodes the value for links.
  • Built-ins: {{COMPANY_NAME}}, {{COMPANY_EMAIL}}, {{COMPANY_PHONE}}, {{WEBSITE}}, {{HEADER_TEXT}}, {{FOOTER_TEXT}}, {{LOGO:raw}}, {{COMPANY_CONTACT:raw}}, {{SENDER_NAME}}, {{SENDER_EMAIL}}, {{EMAIL}}, {{NAME}}.
  • A missing field with no fallback is flagged in the preview, and that email won’t send until it’s fixed.
