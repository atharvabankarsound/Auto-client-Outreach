Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Auto Client Outreach (“the app”) is a free, open-source web page that lets you send personalised emails from your own Gmail account to a list of contacts you upload. It is published as an open-source project on GitHub; questions can be raised via GitHub Issues.

The short version

The app runs entirely in your web browser. It has no server, no database and no analytics. We never receive your spreadsheet, your contacts, your emails or your Google account data.

Google account data

When you click “Connect Gmail”, Google asks you to grant two kinds of access:

  • Your email address and basic profile (openid email profile): used only to show which account is connected, to fill the From name, and to keep per-account send counts in your browser.
  • Send email on your behalf (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send): used only to send the emails you compose and explicitly start sending, including test emails to yourself.

The app cannot read, search, modify or delete any email in your mailbox. The access token Google issues is kept only in the page's memory, expires after about an hour, is discarded when you close the tab and can be revoked at any time with “Disconnect” or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, not sold and not read by humans.

Your spreadsheet and messages

Files you upload are read by your browser and never uploaded anywhere. Each email is sent directly from your browser to Google's Gmail API. Your spreadsheet is not saved; it is gone when you close or reload the page.

What is stored on your device

To be useful between visits, the app keeps the following in your browser's local storage on your device only: your settings (sender name, reply-to, do-not-email list), templates you save, your current draft, and a log of which addresses have been emailed in each campaign (so nobody is emailed twice) plus a daily send count. You can erase all of it in Settings → “Erase everything on this device”, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Third parties

The page loads Google Identity Services (sign-in), Google Fonts and the SheetJS spreadsheet library from their public CDNs. These providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when your browser downloads those files. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Your responsibilities

You are the controller of the contact data you upload and the sender of every email. Make sure you have a lawful basis to contact each person (in the UK, PECR and UK GDPR apply to electronic marketing), identify yourself, and honour opt-out requests.

Changes and contact
